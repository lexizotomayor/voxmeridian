Papua New Guinea's resource sector reported a strong quarter, with gold production and LNG exports both contributing to a notable rise in earnings.

Government officials credited the gains to sustained investment in mining infrastructure and steady global demand for the country's energy exports.

Treasury officials said the country remains focused on diversifying its economy beyond extractive industries.
