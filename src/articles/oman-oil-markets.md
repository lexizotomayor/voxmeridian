Escalating tension between the US and Gulf states over the ongoing conflict with Iran has put oil markets on edge, with traders bracing for potential disruption to shipping routes through the region.

Energy analysts say any sustained disruption could push crude prices higher within weeks, a shift that would filter through to fuel costs for Pacific economies heavily dependent on imported energy.

Pacific governments and shipping operators are watching the situation closely.
