Defense officials across the region say patrol activity near the Taiwan Strait has intensified in recent weeks, with vessels operating closer to contested lines than in previous months.

Analysts describe the shift as part of a broader campaign of pressure short of open conflict, combining military presence with legal and economic tools.

The increased activity has prompted renewed calls among Pacific security planners for clearer crisis communication channels.
