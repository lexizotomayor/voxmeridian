Patrol activity through the Taiwan Strait has picked up noticeably over the past two
weeks, with regional militaries logging more close-quarters passages than in any
comparable stretch this year.

## What changed

None of the individual patrols is unprecedented on its own. What has analysts watching
closely is the pace — the interval between passages has shortened, which reads less
like routine posture and more like a deliberate signal.

## The regional read

Neighbouring navies say they do not expect a confrontation this month. The concern is
structural rather than immediate: each cycle narrows the margin for a miscalculation a
little further, and nobody has yet proposed a mechanism to widen it back out.
