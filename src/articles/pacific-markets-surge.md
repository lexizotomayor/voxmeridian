Tokyo and Sydney both closed at record highs on Thursday, with resource exporters
leading gains across the region on renewed confidence in trade flows.

## What moved

Analysts pointed to easing shipping costs and a run of stronger-than-expected export
data as the main drivers. The rally was broad rather than concentrated in a single
sector, which traders read as a sign of underlying demand rather than a short squeeze.

## The caveat

The run may not survive a shift in US rate policy. A handful of desks are already
trimming exposure ahead of next month's data, on the view that a large part of this
week's gain was a bet on rates staying where they are.
