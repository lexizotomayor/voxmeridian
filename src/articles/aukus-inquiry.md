An independent public inquiry into the AUKUS submarine partnership opened its first hearing in Melbourne, drawing testimony from defense analysts, former officials, and members of the public.

Panelists questioned whether the multi-decade timeline and ballooning budget projections still serve Australia's security interests.

The inquiry is expected to hold further public hearings in the coming months.
